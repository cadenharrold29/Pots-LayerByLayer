The Story

Made by hand.
Printed layer by layer.

This store is a Missouri FFA project. Every pot is designed from scratch in Onshape, 3D printed in durable PLA filament, and finished with an airbrushed logo on the bottom. Each design is completely original.

Every order includes a custom cork-bottom coaster — a printed disc with adhesive cork to protect your surfaces and keep things looking clean. Built-in drainage holes keep your plants healthy.

Filament comes from Sunlu — Bone White and Olive Green are available now. Terra Cotta is on the way.

Plant Care

What to Plant in Yours

Every pot has built-in drainage, so it's ready for a real plant — not just dried arrangements. Here's what tends to do best.

🌵
Spike & Weave (4.5" pots)

Great for succulents, echeveria, small snake plants, or pothos cuttings — anything that likes to dry out between waterings.

🪴
Diamond (5" pot)

Room for a small pothos, philodendron, herb, or a young spider plant. Repot up a size once roots reach the edges.

💧
Watering Tip

Because of the drainage holes, water until it runs out the bottom into your cork coaster, then let the top inch of soil dry before watering again.

☀️
Light

Most beginner-friendly houseplants (pothos, snake plants, succulents) do well in bright, indirect light — no direct grow lamp needed.
